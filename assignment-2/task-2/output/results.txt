STEP 1: STATE
Do midfielders commit more fouls per 90 minutes than defenders
at the FIFA World Cup 2026?

Players in the file: 1039
Players who never played: 33
Goalkeepers: 62

STEP 2: PLAN
H0: mean fouls per 90 is the same for midfielders and defenders
Ha: mean fouls per 90 is not the same
Two sample independent t-test, alpha = 0.05

Outliers using the 1.5 x IQR rule:
  before the cut off: 32 out of 722
  after the cut off:  12 out of 570
Defenders in the population: 268
Midfielders in the population: 302
Sample size for each group: 150

STEP 3: SOLVE

Descriptive statistics for fouls per 90:
Defenders   mean = 0.96  median = 0.94  SD = 0.69  variance = 0.48  IQR = 0.93  range = 3.08
Midfielders mean = 1.26  median = 1.17  SD = 0.9  variance = 0.8  IQR = 0.94  range = 6.25

The mean is bigger than the median in both groups so both
distributions are skewed to the right.

95% confidence intervals (z* = 1.960):
Defenders   0.96 +/- 0.11  so 0.85 to 1.08
Midfielders 1.26 +/- 0.14  so 1.11 to 1.4

Two sample t-test:
  difference in means = 0.29
  standard error = 0.0925
  t* = 3.16
  degrees of freedom = 149
  p-value = 0.0019

STEP 4: CONCLUDE
p is less than 0.05 so I reject H0.
Midfielders commit about 0.29 more fouls per 90 than defenders,
which is roughly 30 % more.
